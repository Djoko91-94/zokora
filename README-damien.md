# Damien Checkers Game

## Overview
The Damien Checkers Game is a strategic board game for two players, played on an 8x8 grid. Players take turns moving their pieces with the goal of capturing all of the opponent's pieces or blocking them so they cannot move.

## Rules
1. **Setup:** Each player starts with 12 pieces placed on the three rows closest to them. 
2. **Objective:** The objective of the game is to capture all of the opponent's pieces or block them so they cannot move.
3. **Movement:** Players can move their pieces diagonally to an adjacent unoccupied square. Pieces can only move forward until they are "kinged."
4. **Capturing:** If an opponent's piece is adjacent to a player's piece and the square immediately beyond it is unoccupied, the player's piece can jump over the opponent's piece and capture it.
5. **King Pieces:** If a player moves a piece to the last row on the opponent's side, that piece is crowned and becomes a "king." Kings can move both forward and backward.
6. **Winning:** A player wins by capturing all of the opponent's pieces or blocking them.

## How to Play
1. Players take turns moving one piece per turn.
2. Players must capture an opponent's piece if able to do so.
3. Play continues until one player wins.

## Features
- **Two-player mode**: Play against a friend.
- **AI mode**: Play against a computer opponent.
- **Difficulty levels**: Choose from easy, medium, or hard.
- **Save and load game**: Save your progress and continue later.
- **Customizable rules**: Adjust rules to suit your playing style or preferences.
